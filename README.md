# Zest Cam

A free pro camera for Android that helps you recreate any pose.

**[Download the latest release](../../releases/latest)** · Android 11 or newer · 64-bit devices · 32 MB

## What it does

- **Pose guide.** Pick any photo and Zest Cam draws its pose (skeleton), body outline or a pencil sketch over the live camera, then scores how closely you match it. It can shoot by itself once you match.
- **Pro controls.** Manual ISO, shutter speed, exposure, white balance (Kelvin) and focus on a lens-ring dial, plus RAW (DNG) and a live histogram. Tap to focus, or long-press to lock focus and exposure.
- **100× zoom.** Goes past your phone's own zoom limit: locks focus, shortens the shutter against shake, merges the sharpest frames of a burst and enlarges with detail-preserving sharpening.
- **Action pre-capture.** Arm it during a thunderstorm or around fast action: it keeps the last half second in memory and saves the best frame the moment lightning flashes or something moves.
- **43 LUTs.** Built-in film looks, or import your own `.cube` files. Shown live in the viewfinder.
- **Auto Enhance.** Adds detail and picks the sharpest of 3 frames to fight hand shake, without changing your colours.
- **Bracketing, aspect ratios (3:4, 4:5, 1:1, 9:16), grid, timer, and film or camcorder date stamps.**

## Install

1. On your phone, open **Releases** and download the latest `Zest-Cam` APK.
2. Open it. Android asks once to **allow installs from this source** (your browser or Files app). Turn it on, then go back.
3. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect asks to scan the app, tap **Scan app**, then **Install**.

Zest Cam only asks for the camera. Photos are saved to `Pictures/ZestCam`, and nothing leaves your phone.

The SHA-256 of the APK is in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
